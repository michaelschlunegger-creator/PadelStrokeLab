## 1. Executive decision

Padel Master already has a substantial short-set motion logger and useful iPhone motion charts. Public Apple APIs support adding owner heart rate, recovery observations, step/distance estimates and optional location. These additions require participant-safe lifecycle, telemetry and export work; they are not existing app capabilities. The first useful result should be a trustworthy physical recording and measurement report, followed by a small owner-led court experiment. Reliable shot recognition and perceived-contact classification remain research questions.

**PROPOSED recommendation:** retain the owner's verified iPhone 15 Pro/Watch Ultra 2 as the first evidence baseline, use the mandatory iOS/watchOS 27 platform, finish phone-led preparation and immutable wearer identity, then validate the motion path, owner heart-rate/recovery path and a separate ordinary-motion guest path. Add pedometer estimates after their accuracy screen. Keep GPS optional and defer channels that add little to this decision. Investigate the latest Series 12/Ultra 4 capabilities as a separate compatibility tier without requiring a purchase or promising their marketing features through third-party APIs. Apple Intelligence should explain verified facts on iPhone after practice; optional cloud/Watch generation needs a separate decision.

The phone remains the main setup, statistics and history terminal. Setup occurs once per practice; every short set requires fresh Watch placement/fit confirmation and explicit Start. The Watch stays on the playing/racket arm. The black/turquoise/white theme, GitHub source of truth, owner-only Health policy and Astra Extra High foundational work are already decided. They are not questions reopened by this study.

### Claims the product can and cannot make

Wrist acceleration and angular velocity describe the Watch. They do not directly measure whole-body acceleration, racket-head speed, ball speed, impact force, target accuracy or complete biomechanics. Integrating noisy wrist acceleration into metres is not an approved body-distance algorithm. Quaternion orientation does not establish where the ball landed. A hardware high-g range is not proof of the effective delivered clipping limit; store a limit only when established for the exact API/hardware, otherwise unknown.

Use explicit states: not requested, unsupported, permission/restriction unavailable, no accessible samples, starting, observed, stale, interrupted, invalid and excluded from analysis. “Supported” means an API reports capability; “observed” means valid samples arrived; “verified transfer” means specified bytes passed checks; “eligible” means a versioned quality rule passed. None implies the others. A user who denies optional location must still be able to record and review eligible motion. Missing telemetry is never filled with owner history, zeros or interpolated invented measurements.

## 4. Data and ownership architecture

### Preserve evidence; add versioned companions

Keep raw-v1, existing metadata, timelines, labels and `.pslrecording` interpretation byte-compatible. Do not rewrite old files to add participants or telemetry. Old context-free recordings stay **unassigned**, even if Player1 is currently selected. New `TrainingRecordingContext` from participant B binds exact canonical raw metadata bytes/digest, wearer, consent and accepted setup. Preserve all v1.2/v1.3 canonical codec, no-follow I/O, caps, linked-identity fences and failed-ID rules.

**PROPOSED telemetry-v1** is a separately bounded companion namespace, with immutable manifest and chunk identities. Fields include practice/set/recording/context IDs, participant and installation epochs, consent decision, acquisition policy/version, stream kind/unit/frame, source device/API, capability observation, sensor timestamp/time domain, wall/monotonic anchors, receipt time, sample/source identifier and quality flags. Sparse Health/location samples retain their native date intervals and provenance. Do not invent a monotonic timestamp for an old Health sample; record mapping uncertainty. Practice IDs are new telemetry/context identities, not reinterpretations of P1 preparation IDs.

Record per-stream request, API-reported frequency and measured rate separately. Batched requests stay nil. Report interval median/p95/max, observed span, startup latency, callback delay, initial/tail missing periods, active-segment gap duration and valid-window coverage. Define a gap threshold per acquisition mode only after measurement; retain raw interval evidence. A quality window crossing a pause, unknown clock mapping, dropout or suspected saturation is ineligible. Test saturated-axis/flat-top detection with known synthetic cases, but distinguish “suspected clipping” from a proven sensor limit. Chart downsampling never feeds detector training.

Motion source timestamps are seconds since boot. Persist a unique clock domain and anchors; restart creates a new domain. Compare streams by timestamp with explicit alignment tolerance, not array index or callback time. Health/location wall-clock corrections, timezone changes and phone/Watch offset uncertainty become explicit mapping events. Pauses and recovery form separate intervals; never connect a line across an unknown interval. Store arrival delay to diagnose batching, rather than shifting recorded impacts to arrival time.

For each stream, define duplicate key and ordering policy. Preserve the existing raw engine's fail-closed duplicate/backward behavior. New sparse telemetry can accept legitimate delayed/out-of-order samples into a bounded reorder stage before immutable publication; exact duplicates are idempotent, conflicting duplicates quarantined. Declare maximum lateness/queue/bytes before implementation. Late arrivals cannot silently alter a previously exported analysis: publish a new version with source references. Overflow ends or marks the affected channel, never silently converts partial data into complete data.

Store native axes untouched. Add separately versioned transforms for wrist/crown/left-right comparisons, with quaternion multiplication/sign conventions and calibration fixture tests. Use a brief stationary reference and known-direction motion only if physical evidence shows it is useful. A coordinate transform cannot remove anatomical/technique differences or infer actual fit. A changed strap, racket, playing arm, hardware or acquisition mode remains an analysis partition variable.

### Immutable wearer and admission

Training alone exposes local Player1/2/3 slots. Stable pseudonymous participant keys survive slot renaming/reuse; slots are not identities or owner privileges. Every sample, reference label, summary, query, transfer, export and model partition resolves to its immutable context. Owner-attested device relationship is separate from selected slot and label author. A guest sees only acquired guest channels; owner Health/profile/history must never fill gaps. Consent to install or use TestFlight is not consent to collection/model use.

Retain the v1.2 Start linearization: the sole runtime coordinator invalidates pending Start before dispatching any authority mutation; blocks admission until committed registry state is reconciled; freezes subject/policy/consent/setup generations and exact metadata; publishes bound context off the main actor; then checks publication identity, current committed revision, authorization generation and both setup generations. **No suspension may occur between the final checks and capture admission.** Existing/partial context publications cannot restart capture. A store checksum/publication receipt alone never authorizes Start.

A wearer, mode or consent change stops old capture/recovery/history/location observers before a new wearer can Start. Close admission first, fence callback generations, request resource shutdown, await observed acknowledgements/drain, then retire old authority. A timeout/failure leaves Start blocked with an actionable error. `WatchWorkoutDriver.end()` must gain completion semantics in its own bounded package; the current immediate return is insufficient. Late callbacks stay bound to their original context or are discarded with a reason, never attributed to current UI selection. Revoke also invalidates pending AI/query results and caches.

Phone-requested withdrawal/end while offline is **pending**, not applied on Watch. Watch continues under its last actually accepted authority until it receives a valid request or local policy ends the session; UI must state the observation time and pending status. No promise of instantaneous remote revocation. Once applied, durable consent barriers prevent replay, including linked aliases. Resolve the human consent/retention process before any non-owner recording.

## 5. Practice heart rate, recovery and guest feasibility

### Owner practice lifecycle — proposed

Use one explicitly started owner practice resource with set-level motion segments, rather than ending its Health session at every set. Proposed states: ready → starting → active set → between-set recovery → ready for next set → ending → ended/interrupted. An explicit practice pause is a separate state; it must not masquerade as measured recovery. Current 120-active-second motion sets remain bounded until a separate duration gate approves expansion.

**PROPOSED recovery design:** keep the legitimate owner workout running during a short, visibly labelled between-set rest so HR can be observed, while stopping high-rate motion outside active set windows. Do not call `HKWorkoutSession.pause()` and assume HR continues at workout cadence. An explicit Pause practice pauses the workout and makes recovery unavailable/partial unless actual samples support it. End practice ends all resources; process recovery ends the prior resource, marks missing data and requires fresh preparation. Another app's workout, permission loss or Watch removal causes explicit interruption. No hidden indefinite workout to gain runtime.

**PROPOSED windows for review:** pre-set baseline is median available HR in the preceding30s; report its count/time span, never manufacture it when too sparse. End-set reference is a median in the last10s. Report observed HR difference around +60s and +120s using a ±10s window, at least two accessible samples per window, and actual sample times/counts. If the next set starts first, observation stops and the later window is missing. Display signed difference (HR can rise) and time toward observed baseline; no assumed monotonic fall or medical cutoff. These windows are analysis defaults requiring approval, not Apple-defined recovery or validated physiology.

Live builder statistics may aggregate samples; preserve source-level HR samples/intervals needed by this definition via an appropriate authorized query, deduplicated by sample identity. Do not manufacture individual points from a cumulative average. Characterize cadence and timestamps first. Persist only expressly selected owner channels; choose saving/discarding an actual workout in §10. A builder/session architecture does not by itself authorize saving Health samples or querying unrelated history. An active workout can cause the system to generate Health samples/activity effects even when this app reads no types, creates no builder or saves no workout summary. ## 6. Apple Intelligence and automatic recognition are separate systems

Signal recognition first needs an event detector with non-shot/unknown separation, then supported stroke classification, then a separately justified perceived-contact model. A language model is not the sensor classifier. Transparent deterministic features/rules are an appropriate first baseline; Core ML may later run a validated classifier on iPhone or Watch if it earns its resource cost. No current source contains that classifier.

For explanations, the installed iOS27 FoundationModels interface exposes `SystemLanguageModel`, runtime availability, supported locales, context size/token counting, structured generation and tools. Apple's canonical documentation identifies it as an on-device model. The Watch27 interface explicitly marks **SystemLanguageModel unavailable on watchOS** at lines256–259; the presence of a FoundationModels framework on Watch does not imply local LLM inference. [SystemLanguageModel](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/foundationmodels/systemlanguagemodel).

OS27 adds `PrivateCloudComputeLanguageModel` on iOS/watchOS27, confirmed at Watch interface lines43–45. This is server inference with network, eligibility, managed entitlement and per-user quota dependencies. Current Apple access requires Small Business Program enrollment, fewer than2million first-time App Store downloads and entitlement assignment; this app's eligibility is **unverified**. No unconditional free/cloud/offline promise. [PCC API](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/foundationmodels/privatecloudcomputelanguagemodel), [access conditions](https://developer.apple.com/private-cloud-compute/), [WWDC26/241](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/241/).

**PROPOSED default:** post-practice iPhone-local explanations from a small validated facts object; Watch can display a short factual cached result. No generative model in logging, Start/Stop, permission, wearer or safety decisions. No automatic PCC/external-provider fallback. Optional PCC/Watch generation remains a later package requiring privacy/cost/owner approval. Built-in Workout Buddy/Siri features do not grant the app access to their algorithms or prove its own generated advice.

The owner's iPhone15Pro is in Apple's eligible hardware list, but actual language, region, model download/readiness and settings were not inspected. Handle all unavailable reasons, unsupported locale, model not ready, restrictions, cancellation, refusal, timeout and resource limits. Apple's consumer language/region eligibility is separate from the model's runtime `supportsLocale`; do not infer availability from the device model alone. Check `contextSize`/`tokenCount` on the running version and reserve output space; avoid a permanent4096-token assumption. OS/model updates require new evaluation. [Apple eligibility](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121115), [model capabilities](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/foundationmodels/systemlanguagemodel).

Generation input contains only eligible subject-scoped measurements, coverage/uncertainty, compatible-session comparisons and approved routine IDs. Use guided structured output (`Generable`/schema constraints) and read-only tools over a bounded facts store; validate IDs, facts and units after generation. Prefer deterministic rendering of numbers, allowing the model to choose/explain supported findings. User notes are untrusted data, never instructions. The model cannot create samples, alter labels, select another subject, change goals, send/upload or prescribe technique/medical advice. Owner-approved routines remain suggestions until the owner chooses them.

Fallback templates must show the same facts and familiar approved practice instructions when AI is unavailable or rejected. Show why a comparison is unavailable, not a plausible invented trend. Run generation after capture; cancel or defer under battery/thermal pressure. Offline local generation is conditional on model readiness; downloading requires connectivity. PCC is always a distinct network-dependent path. Acceptance includes missing facts, all-uncertain strokes, conflicting sessions, prompt injection in notes, changed participant during generation, fabricated numbers/diagnoses, disabled/unready AI and OS/model changes. Any unsupported measurement or subject leak blocks release.
