## Source conclusions and ratified rules

The non-Sendable synchronous channel owns both its journal and O2 privately. Watch sequences authority; phone persists one intent and mirrors the exact accepted transition stream. Every append precedes O2 application, and every result/ACK follows verification of the actual resulting authority. Reopen validates the canonical bounded chain and locates O2 at an exact anchor/intermediate/tip node; it never promotes an uncommitted temporary. A temporary is preserved and blocks opening. Known installations must use `openExisting`; missing or corrupt state is not recreated.

Interrupted O2 practice retirement is handled explicitly: only the committed retirement plus its exact before-state and existing guard justify one recovery advancement. Already-applied transitions do not advance again. Fresh repeated practice retirement against an old guard is rejected. Watch ACK pruning advances only an exact retained contiguous prefix and its durable anchor/high-water. Reordered, conflicting, foreign and missing-cursor replay cannot silently advance or prune authority.

The phone's none/HR/all fence is a bounded local privacy restriction, not a second command queue or evidence of remote application. Conflicting narrowing persists the stronger fence and returns `intentOutstanding`. A sent intent cannot be erased by cancellation, timeout or a lost reply. Explicit obsolete-intent resolution requires the retained exact-chain proof; explicit satisfied-fence resolution requires current verified denial and no pending intent. Exact matching restrictive completion may clear only the covered fence. Status, End and unrelated grants do not release it. All director-ratified refinements are recorded in the final evidence document, including the dispatched-enrollment exception and bounded restrictive-history compaction.

The journal retains at most 256 compact transitions within 1 MiB, plus a bounded O1 anchor represented inside that envelope. Ordinary work preserves three distinct reserves; only a fresh practice retirement, actual HR true-to-false or full withdrawal can spend the corresponding reserve. Generic advancement, redundant false and repeated retirement do not gain extra reserve capacity. Tests cover real publication/apply/reopen/ACK/prune and request/dispatch/cancel/obsolete/fence/checkpoint boundaries, namespace integrity, raw/legacy sibling preservation and context-store poison propagation.
