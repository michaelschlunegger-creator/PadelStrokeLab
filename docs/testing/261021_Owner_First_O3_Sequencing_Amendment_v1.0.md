## One sequencer and bounded convergence

After explicit paired revision1 enrollment, **Watch alone sequences authority transitions**. Phone persists at most one outstanding authority intent and immediately blocks its own pending admission/export when a restrictive intent is requested. It does not apply that intent to its authority mirror before an exact accepted Watch transition. Latest draft suggestion remains separately coalesced; it is never preparation or consent acceptance.

Watch retains each explicit transition until phone durably mirrors and acknowledges it. A transition identifies owner and exact phone/Watch installation epochs, monotonically increasing stream sequence, immutable command identity, exact before-authority digest, typed operation and expected resulting authority digest. Supported operations are initial enrollment, optional HR change, withdrawal, explicit re-consent, revision-only retirement and practice retirement with its minimal replay guard. O1 constructors/transition functions reconstruct and validate the result. No decoded status/snapshot imports authority, transfers grants, resets barriers or changes wearer identity.

The journal keeps one exact acknowledged anchor authority and compact typed commands, rather than repeating two complete identities per transition. Replaying the anchor plus the ordered commands must reproduce every before/after digest and the current store exactly. Unrelated epochs, skipped/reordered sequences, equal-sequence conflicting commands, equal authority revisions with different bytes, unsupported policy and inconsistent chain nodes fail closed. One owner relationship is supported; peer replacement is not an automatic migration.

Example: both devices hold revision1; Watch End records/applies explicit revision1→2 while offline; phone retains a withdrawal intent without changing its mirror. On reconnect phone first mirrors the retained End transition. It may then rebuild that restrictive intent against exact revision2 only after verifying that all intervening steps were revision-only and the same consent generation/withdrawal barrier still applies. Watch applies revision2→3; phone mirrors that exact accepted transition. The original human decision ID/time remain evidence. Grants/re-consent never automatically rebase across a barrier. Changed consent generation/barrier requires explicit resolution; pending restriction remains visible and locally blocked.

O3b owns durable pending-phone-intent creation, dispatch marking and resolution/clear, together with its one-intent rule. O4/O5 consume that API and implement immediate local blocking; they do not add another intent-persistence mechanism. Pending full withdrawal blocks admission, analysis and export; pending HR-disable blocks new HR analysis/export as well as later HR admission. Dispatch is marked durably before transport submission. Only a definitely undispatched intent may be cancelled/cleared locally; after dispatch, lost reply or UI cancellation does not erase an unresolved authority action or locally unblock it. An exact durably mirrored transition resolves its matching intent. A lost reply, timeout, status observation, draft delivery or transfer completion cannot acknowledge a consent change or permit Start. Phone narrowing requests remain visibly pending until Watch applies/acknowledges them. Watch-local End still shuts down its resources if persistence fails; failure blocks renewed preparation/admission and reports the unresolved durable state.

## Durable journal and finite limits

- Wire maximum: **32KiB** per canonical owner-setup message. Maximum O1 history must fit status and narrowing/withdrawal messages without duplicated full identities. No raw samples or HR samples enter this channel.
- Journal: at most **256 retained unacknowledged transitions**, canonical envelope at most **1MiB**, compact individual transition at most **4KiB**. The acknowledged O1 anchor is separately bounded by its existing16KiB limit. Initial enrollment carries its bounded revision1 identity; no history import is permitted.
- Pending phone authority intents: **one**. A second conflicting intent requires resolving/cancelling the first; exact retries are idempotent. Ordinary draft edits coalesce independently and invalidate stale preparation replies.
- Ordinary authority work/new-practice admission must leave count and byte capacity for **End, actual HR-disable and full withdrawal**. These three distinct reserved transitions can each consume its reservation once until acknowledged; redundant restrictions or repeated retirement cannot consume another reserved slot. Tests must exercise all valid ordering permutations at the boundary. If any limit/durability check prevents recording a restriction, source shutdown still runs and admission remains blocked; no acknowledgement is invented.
- One atomically replaced checksummed journal file with an exclusive no-follow lock, bounded regular temporary and explicit create-new/open-existing intent. Existing or missing/corrupt state is never silently reset. No raw/owner-context payload deletion is part of this journal.

Sequence transaction: validate the exact current chain tip and command; durably append the transition; apply the exact O2 operation; verify actual resulting bytes/digest; then expose an applied transition/acknowledgement. Any ambiguous write poisons the current owner. Reopen validates the full committed chain and locates actual O2 authority at its exact anchor/intermediate/tip node before applying any remaining committed transition. Uncommitted journal temporaries never authorize authority changes. A transition is never acknowledged merely because an O2 method returned successfully.

The O2 interrupted-retirement case is explicit: its guard may exist while authority remains at the transition's before-state. Journal recovery verifies that exact guard and before-state and performs the one expected revision advancement. If authority is already the exact after-state, replay is metadata-idempotent. Any other state blocks; arbitrary advancement is forbidden.

Phone mirrors only the next exact transition from its known chain anchor. It durably records receipt before O2 application and sends an exact sequence/result/transition acknowledgement only after persistence and reconciliation. Watch prunes only an acknowledged contiguous prefix, atomically advancing the retained anchor/high-water. Missing entries cannot be interpreted as an empty queue. Duplicate acknowledgements and lost acknowledgements retain safe replay; older transitions cannot overwrite the mirror. Relaunch requires fresh preparation generations independently of journal recovery.
