## Implementation review refinements (before affected regression changes)

- Permission setup is a separate explicit maximum30-second stage after the workout runs and before the capture engine opens its first clock window. Its authorization samples are excluded from capture. Only then does the five-second actual-data watchdog start. Reported frequencies are labeled in capability metadata; sensor amplitude limits remain unknown (`knownSensorLimit=nil`), and no sampling rate is requested.
- Capture window exclusions are explicit: valid pre-window or post-cutoff samples are counted separately; the pause/stop timeline details retain cumulative exclusion counts. No timestamp is rewritten, and backwards/duplicate timestamps within the eligible window fail closed. Terminal tail remains unknown even when no excluded samples were observed.
- Admission and dispatch share one ingress transaction with control cutoff and dispatch. Auto duration/chunk limits enqueue finalization behind all already admitted batches; they cannot finalize inline from a producer's worker turn. Stop/failure permanently seals admission so a pending begin/resume cannot reopen it.
- The tighter per-stream queued/executing admission cap is4,096 samples. Checkpoint before live partial buffers exceed4,095 samples. Therefore at a limit boundary, each terminal buffer is at most8,191 samples (partial plus all admitted samples), below device motion's9,361-sample and acceleration's32,764-sample per-chunk bounds. Two terminal chunk slots suffice. The whole mailbox still enforces eight batches,16,384 samples and2MiB. Tests explicitly exercise an8,191-sample device-motion terminal chunk at255 total chunks.
- Resume is disabled until both durable engine pause and actual HealthKit paused acknowledgement arrive. The pause gate resets on terminal, new-set, start and resume. Unexpected HealthKit pauses interrupt. A five-second transition watchdog also covers requested workout pause.
- Core Motion capture handlers are constructed by nonisolated factories and explicitly Sendable; they never assume callback delivery on MainActor. Delayed generation-tagged errors after pause/stop or across resume cannot interrupt another window. Lifecycle errors can still interrupt a paused session.
- Retained-list refresh is blocked during active capture. The current active recording cannot be manually transferred before terminal sealing. A stopped pre-capture permission flow has no raw recording and is never queued for transfer. Recovery notices update through app storage and can be dismissed.

Added regressions: concurrent receive-vs-stop retains every accepted pre-cutoff batch; blocked resume persistence cannot reopen sealed admission; source errors after close/across generation; automatic-limit drain retains an already queued other-stream tail; maximum reserved tail bound; actual-store interrupted-prefix inspection; rapid pause/resume and new-set pause reset.
